Privacy Policy

onedrive-exelearning is a static web application that opens, edits, and saves eXeLearning .elpx projects directly in your Microsoft OneDrive.

What we store

The app stores nothing on a server. There is no backend. The access token issued by the Microsoft identity platform lives in your browser tab's memory and is dropped when you close the tab.

What we access

With your consent we request the minimum Microsoft Graph delegated permissions required to open, save, and list the eXeLearning files you choose: Files.ReadWrite and User.Read.

Third parties

Network traffic goes directly from your browser to Microsoft Graph (graph.microsoft.com) and the Microsoft identity platform (login.microsoftonline.com). The app does not relay or proxy any traffic.

Contact

This is an open-source project; report issues at github.com/exelearning/onedrive-exelearning.
